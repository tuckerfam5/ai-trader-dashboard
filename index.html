Return since start

Indexed to 0% at the account's first day, so a $100,000 account and a $1,000 account can be compared fairly — against each other and against just holding the S&P 500.

Performance

Closed trades only, measured in R — one R is the amount the stop-loss put at risk on that trade. Expectancy is the average R per trade: positive means the rules make money over time, whatever any one pick did.

Current positions

What each account is holding right now, the exits resting at the broker for each, and the thesis it was bought on. A starred price is the plan on file with no order actually resting at the broker.

Cycle journal

The portfolio manager's and risk manager's overall thinking each cycle — including anything the risk manager killed before it reached the market.

Trade log

Every order the AI actually placed (or was blocked from placing), in its own words. Click one to read the reasoning.

Paper trading only — no real money is ever at risk here. Data refreshes each scheduled trading cycle.
